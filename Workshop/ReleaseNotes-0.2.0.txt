Initial public release of Selective ReTend for RimWorld 1.6.

- Re-tend infections, diseases and injuries with independent settings.
- Infection → disease → injury priority.
- Configurable target quality and number of attempts.
- Different medicine policies for dangerous conditions and ordinary injuries.
- Generic support for tended conditions that do not develop immunity.
- Safeguards against accumulated-treatment, duration and medical-XP exploits.
- English and Spanish translations.
